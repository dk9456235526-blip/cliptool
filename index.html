Position

Four steps from long video to short clips.

Everything happens on your device. Your video is never uploaded anywhere.

01

Upload

Drop in a video

The browser opens the file locally and reads its length and resolution.

02

Set up

Choose length, count and frame

Pick a clip length, how many clips, and a 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9 crop.

03

Analyze

Find the best moments

The audio is scored every half second for loudness and spikes, then the top non-overlapping windows are picked and snapped to quiet points.

04

Refine

Name, trim and preview

Rename clips in bulk, adjust start and end, add your own, and preview each one.

No uploads, no accounts, no storage: close the tab and it is gone.
